# CircleFound launch operations

## Application statuses
new → reviewing → accepted → checkout_sent → paid → placed
Alternative: waitlisted, declined, rematch_requested, closed.

## Acceptance email
Subject: You’re accepted to the CircleFound founding cohort

You’re accepted. Complete your $9 founding placement using the secure page below and use the same name and email from your application:
https://circlefound-social.vercel.app/accepted

## Decline/waitlist email
Subject: CircleFound application update

Thank you for applying. We do not have a suitable founding-circle placement available yet. You have not been charged. We will retain your application only as described in our Privacy Notice.

## Group introduction
Introduce first names only, state the shared fit reasons, repeat no-dating/no-selling rules, provide the first activity options, and ask everyone to confirm before sharing contact details.

## Incident handling
1. Preserve the report privately.
2. Separate involved members.
3. Remove access for threats, harassment, fraud, dating solicitation, or sales activity.
4. Direct immediate danger to emergency services.
5. Record the decision and delete unnecessary sensitive data.
